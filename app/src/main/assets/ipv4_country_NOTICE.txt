ipv4_country.bin.gz - offline IPv4 to country table used by the peer list.

Source: free IP to Country Lite database by DB-IP (https://db-ip.com),
distributed through https://github.com/sapics/ip-location-db and licensed under
the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International License.

The table is generated by tools/build-geoip-asset.py: every IPv4 range of the
source database becomes one record (start address delta plus a country index),
gzip-compressed and bundled inside the APK. POCKETCORE looks addresses up
locally and never contacts a GeoIP service, so the addresses of the peers this
node talks to never leave the phone.

IP Geolocation by DB-IP: https://db-ip.com
