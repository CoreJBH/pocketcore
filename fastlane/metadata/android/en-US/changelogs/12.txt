versionCode 12 - 1.0.0

- The bundled bitcoind is checked against a SHA-256 recorded at build time
  before it runs, and the build script verifies Bitcoin Core's signed
  SHA256SUMS.asc.
- Chain data, the peer cache and the node settings are excluded from cloud
  backup and from device-to-device transfer.
- The RPC password is only sent to a socket the daemon itself holds.
- A daemon left over from a killed app is stopped gracefully; it is only
  killed when that stop fails.
- Optional Tor routing through Orbot (127.0.0.1:9050).
- Every connected peer is listed instead of the first six.
- Watch-only descriptors and the debug log are copied as sensitive clipboard
  items, so the system hides their contents.
