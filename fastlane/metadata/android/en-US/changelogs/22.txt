versionCode 22 - 1.0.0

- Built for Android 16 (API level 36).
- Exporting a UTXO snapshot now checks the free space before the dump
  starts and says how much is missing, instead of failing after minutes.
- If Android refuses the foreground service that keeps the node alive, the
  NODE tab now says so, so a node that may be stopped in the background is
  never mistaken for a protected one.
