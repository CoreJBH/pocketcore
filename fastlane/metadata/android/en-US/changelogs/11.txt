versionCode 11 - 1.0.0

- The RPC password is never stored in the clear: if the device Keystore
  cannot encrypt it, it is kept in memory for that session only.
- A new RPC password is generated on every start, and the RPC and P2P ports
  are held until the node is launched.
- Cancelled scans and lookups stop cleanly instead of reporting a failure.
- A version check that does not return is stopped instead of left running.
- The node notification shows no contents on the lock screen.
- Smaller download: release builds are minified.
